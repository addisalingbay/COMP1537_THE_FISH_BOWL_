FISH BOWL

Welcome to THE FISH BOWL!

Discover a world of colourful sea life at The Fish Bowl! Opened in 2001, the aquarium is known for its enormous goldfish, which can grow up to ten times larger than the average household goldfish. Guests can explore underwater tunnels and exhibits featuring sea turtles, sharks, jellyfish, and tropical fish. The aquarium also hosts guided tours, animal feedings, and conservation programs.
